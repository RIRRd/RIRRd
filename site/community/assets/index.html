Reviewed submissions

What happens next?

  1. Your submission enters a private review queue.
  2. The operator and public endpoints are checked before publication.
  3. Approved listings credit the operator, hosting provider and named sponsor.

Your contact email and reviewer notes are not included in the public directory. Submissions are removed by an hourly cleanup after 90 days. Endpoints are not fetched automatically from this form.

Submitting a listing does not require payment, create a sponsorship contract or give access to another registry’s objects.
