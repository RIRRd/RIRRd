# Community conduct

RIRRd welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Human and AI-assisted contributions are welcome under the same review and test
requirements.

Be respectful, patient and constructive. Explain technical disagreements with
reproducible examples. Credit other people's work and preserve its license and
provenance. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and deliberate abuse of
community infrastructure are unacceptable.

Keep credentials, private registry records and other people's personal details
out of issues, pull requests and discussions. Never test updates against public
registries without the operator's authorization. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
for suspected vulnerabilities.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove inappropriate content, close abusive
threads and restrict participation to protect contributors. Decisions should be
proportionate and based on behavior. Use GitHub's content reporting tools for
abuse; do not retaliate or publish private information.

This policy applies to the project's repositories, issue trackers and community
spaces. It does not create a support, uptime or security-response guarantee.
